Craftsmanship

HIEU NGUYEN DUC

Master Trouser Maker

Three Trousers. One Very Long Day.

There was a time when Nguyen Duc Hieu could spend as many as 18 hours at his workbench.

At the end of that day, he might have completed only three pairs of trousers.

Not thirty.

DOAN TRAN VAN

Handcrafted Suit Jackets

As a young apprentice, he followed the traditional path into the trade — observing experienced tailors, assisting with their work, practicing repeatedly, and gradually learning to understand a garment not only through measurements, but through his hands.

He later trained and worked as an apprentice at Phu Hung Tailor in Hanoi, where he continued developing his skills in traditional tailoring.

It was the kind of education that cannot be rushed.

DUY DAT NGUYEN

Founder & CEO

In 2009, I opened a small tailoring shop in Hanoi with a simple belief: if we wanted customers to trust us, we had to make clothes we were genuinely proud of.

We did not have a large factory, a famous name, or sophisticated technology. What we had was craftsmanship, persistence, and a willingness to remake a garment when it was not good enough.

That principle has stayed with me ever since.
