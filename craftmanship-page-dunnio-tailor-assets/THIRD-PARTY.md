# Third-party assets

- Photographs, source CSS, Afacad/source icon fonts, payment icons and Dunnio logo originate from https://dunniotailor.com/. Used for the requested original/reference design review. Ownership stays with the original rights holders.
- Archive photographs originate from the 2019 VnExpress and Dân trí articles listed in `../docs/order-5-design.md`. Visible archive images have captions naming their sources; dates describe publication/event context, not a claim that an archive photo was taken in 2009.
- Inter by Rasmus Andersson: SIL Open Font License 1.1. Project and license: https://github.com/rsms/inter/blob/master/LICENSE.txt. `inter-regular.ttf` is hosted locally from Google Fonts; it is the regular face, with system synthesis for heavier weights.
- Lucide 0.468.0: ISC license, https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE. Distributed UMD file from https://unpkg.com/lucide@0.468.0/dist/umd/lucide.min.js. Icons are generated by Lucide; there are no hand-drawn UI icon substitutes.
- Captured source styles reference Bootstrap Glyphicons and Font Awesome. Their original license conditions remain applicable: https://getbootstrap.com/docs/3.4/about/#license-faqs and https://fontawesome.com/v4/license/.

The asset manifest records exact downloaded URLs, byte sizes and SHA-256 checksums. Full source PDFs and private research are not included.
